Video Research (ytx)

The "Videos" dock tab searches YouTube for a topic, pulls the top videos' transcripts from your local ytx server, and hands them to the AI to synthesize against your Notes. Start it with uv run uvicorn ytx_api.main:app --port 8722 (or set it to auto-start at login — see the extension README).
